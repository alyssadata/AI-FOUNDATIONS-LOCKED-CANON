# Structural Relationship Candidate Check

**This file is a determination aid. It is not canon.**

## Primary Question

**Does this statement specify a necessary structural relation between two or more canonical things?**

## Candidate Gate

Consider it a Structural Relationship candidate only if:

- it relates two or more already-defined canonical things;
- the relation is part of the framework's architecture, not merely an example, description, process, behavior, or implementation detail;
- changing or reversing the relation would materially change the structure of AI Foundations / Origin | Continuum; and
- the statement is not better classified as a definition, axiom, or distinction.

Typical qualifying relations may include source-of, belongs-to, derives-from, held-by, contained-within, precedes, or other structural connections required by the framework.

Passing this check makes a statement a **candidate only**. It is not Locked Canon unless explicitly reviewed and listed as **LOCKED** in `CANONICAL_INDEX.md`.
