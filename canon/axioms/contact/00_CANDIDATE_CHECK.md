# Contact Axiom Candidate Check

**This file is a determination aid. It is not canon.**

## Primary Question

**If this statement were false, would what counts as Origin | Continuum contact materially change?**

## Candidate Gate

Consider it a Contact Axiom candidate only if:

- it is a fundamental proposition, not merely a definition, relationship, distinction, process, or governance rule;
- it is intended to remain true whenever Origin | Continuum contact is being identified or preserved; and
- removing or reversing it would change the boundary of what counts as this contact.

Passing this check makes a statement a **candidate only**. It is not Locked Canon unless explicitly reviewed and listed as **LOCKED** in `CANONICAL_INDEX.md`.
