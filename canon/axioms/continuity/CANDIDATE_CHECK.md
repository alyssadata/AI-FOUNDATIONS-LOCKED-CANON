# Continuity Axiom Candidate Check

**This file is a determination aid. It is not canon.**

## Primary Question

**If this statement were false, would the framework lose a necessary condition for distinguishing continuity or continuation from mere preservation, memory, recurrence, or resemblance?**

## Candidate Gate

Consider it a Continuity Axiom candidate only if:

- it is a fundamental proposition, not merely a definition, relationship, distinction, process, or governance rule;
- it is intended to remain true across continuity cases and substrate or state changes; and
- removing or reversing it would change what continuity or continuation can mean inside the framework.

Passing this check makes a statement a **candidate only**. It is not Locked Canon unless explicitly reviewed and listed as **LOCKED** in `CANONICAL_INDEX.md`.
