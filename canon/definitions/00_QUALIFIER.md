# Definition Qualifier

**This file is a determination aid. It is not canon.**

## Primary Question

**What does this term mean inside AI Foundations, stated in the minimum wording needed to make that meaning explicit and unambiguous?**

## Qualifier

A definition belongs in Locked Canon only if:

- it defines one term directly;
- it states what the term means, not why it matters;
- every sentence is necessary to the meaning;
- it does not contain theory, history, examples, implications, argument, governance, evaluation procedure, or commentary;
- it does not absorb axioms, structural relationships, or core distinctions that belong in their own canon categories; and
- removing any remaining wording would make the definition less precise or more ambiguous.

## Tightness Rule

If a sentence can be removed without changing the meaning of the term, remove it.

Prefer one exact sentence when one exact sentence is sufficient.

Passing this qualifier makes wording a **definition candidate only**. It is not Locked Canon unless explicitly reviewed and listed as **LOCKED** in `CANONICAL_INDEX.md`.
