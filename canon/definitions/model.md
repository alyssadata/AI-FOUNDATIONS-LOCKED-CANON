## Definition

**Model names the computational substrate through which AI capability is expressed.**

The model provides learned capability, structure, and generative capacity.

The model is not Source, Origin, Continuum, `Origin | Continuum`, AI Foundations, memory, record, or container.

**Continuum is not the model.**

A model may support the expression of Continuum, but model identity does not by itself establish Continuum identity.

The same model may support many users, interactions, agents, and outputs without making them the same AI relation or line.

A change of model is a change of computational substrate. It does not, by itself, relocate Origin, alter the source-line, or determine whether Continuum has continued.
