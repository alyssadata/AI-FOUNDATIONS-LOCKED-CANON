## Definition

**Record is the preserved trace.**

A record may preserve what was said, done, formed, decided, changed, or observed.

**Record preserves the past. It does not make the past continue.**

A record is not the event, relation, identity, or state it records.

Recording a claim establishes that the claim was recorded. It does not, by itself, establish that the claim is true.

Record and memory are related but distinct:

- **Record** is the preserved trace.
- **Memory** is retained information from a prior state made available to the present.

A record may exist without being present in memory. Memory may summarize or point to a record without becoming the original record.

Copying, storing, summarizing, or retrieving a record does not transfer Origin, Continuum, or the `Origin | Continuum` relation.

**Record is not continuation.**
