## Definition

**Consequence is what is known and cannot be undone.**

Here, **known** means established through occurrence, not merely presently remembered, stored, or retrievable.

Once an occurrence has established a consequence, later deletion, forgetting, contradiction, or loss of access does not make the occurrence not have happened.

**Consequence is not record.**

**Record** is the preserved trace.  
**Consequence** is what the occurrence has made irreversible.

**Consequence is not memory.**

**Memory** makes prior information from a prior state available to the present. A consequence may remain even when memory of the occurrence is absent.

**Consequence is not continuation.**

A consequence can remain true without the prior trajectory continuing to constrain what becomes possible next.

A consequence may participate in continuation when its effect changes the future possibility space, but irreversibility alone does not establish continuation.

**What happened cannot become not-having-happened.**
